Multi-Law-Firm Architecture: Inbound Routing, AI Isolation & Multi-Clio Integration

How a Single Voice Agent Platform Simultaneously Serves Multiple Independent Law Firms with Strict Data Isolation
Target Platform: Multi-Tenant Voice Agent Core <—> Independent Clio Accounts Architecture Mode: Multi-Tenant SaaS / Agency Provider

1. Executive Summary: The Multi-Firm Challenge

When running a Voice Agent platform for multiple different law firms (e.g., Apex Legal Group, Bottini Law, and Gotham Partners), the system must solve three mission-critical problems:

  1. Inbound Identification: When a phone call arrives, how does the Voice Agent know which law firm is being called?
  2. AI Brand & Persona Isolation: The agent must greet the caller with the correct firm name, attorney roster, and practice areas.
  3. Data Isolation & Multi-Clio Dispatch: The caller's contact, transcript, and legal matter must route strictly to that specific firm's Clio account, with zero risk of Firm A seeing Firm B's clients.
Core Architectural Principle: The Dialed Phone Number (DID) is the universal routing key. Every law firm owns a distinct inbound phone number. When a call arrives, the Voice Agent matches the dialed number against a multi-tenant registry, loads that firm's specific AI prompt, and selects that firm's dedicated Clio OAuth token for post-call sync.

2. How the Voice Agent Identifies the Law Firm (DID Matching)

In enterprise telephony, every phone number is known as a Direct Inward Dialing (DID) number. Whether a firm uses Twilio, Telnyx, or LiveKit SIP trunks, the carrier's incoming SIP INVITE always contains two essential headers:

  • From: The caller's personal number (e.g., +1-555-019-3388).
  • To: The dialed phone number that belongs to the law firm (e.g., +1-555-222-0001).

5. How Each Law Firm Connects Their Clio Account (The OAuth Flow)

You do not need to manually collect passwords from your law firms. Each firm connects securely via Clio OAuth 2.0:

Step 1: Send the Firm Their Unique Onboarding Link

When you onboard Bottini Law, your platform generates a custom authorization link containing a unique state parameter: 

https://app.clio.com/oauth/authorize?response_type=code&client_id=1zYn1AJ48HtBvdTZz3OOLljEqPKYhHQZKAWK11tz&redirect_uri=https://dashboard-production-55c4.up.railway.app/oauth/callback&state=org_bottini_02

Step 2: Managing Partner Clicks "Allow Access"

The managing partner at Bottini Law logs into their own Clio account and clicks Allow Access.

Step 3: Callback Stores Tokens to That Specific Firm

Clio redirects back to your dashboard with both the authorization code and the state: /oauth/callback?code=AUTH_CODE&state=org_bottini_02. Your server exchanges the code and saves the resulting token under org_bottini_02.

6. Adding Firm #2, Firm #3, etc. (Step-by-Step Operator Checklist)

Operator Checklist for Onboarding a New Law Firm:

Step 1: Create the Organization in Voice Agent:
Go to your Voice Agent dashboard at /organizations and click Create Organization (e.g., "Bottini Law").

Step 2: Assign a Dedicated Phone Number:
Go to Telephony > Buy Phone Number or assign an existing DID to that organization.

Step 3: Configure Practice-Specific AI Prompt:
In Agent Builder, set the firm's custom greeting, business hours, and intake questions.

Step 4: Connect the Firm's Clio Account:
Generate their unique OAuth connect link. Once authorized, all calls to their dedicated phone number automatically route into their private Clio account!

7. Security, Legal Ethics & Malpractice Prevention

  • Zero Data Bleed (Strict Multi-Tenancy): Every database query and Clio API dispatch strictly filters by workspace_id. There is no shared memory between active calls.
  • Independent Conflict Checking: When an intake call arrives for Firm A, the conflict check only searches Firm A's Clio database, ensuring strict compliance with attorney-client confidentiality and ABA Model Rule 1.6.
  • Independent Client Portals: When Firm A invites Mary, she sees Firm A's logo and legal team in her portal. Firm B's clients see Firm B's branding.
