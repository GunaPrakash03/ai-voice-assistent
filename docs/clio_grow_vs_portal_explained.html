1. Why is the Word "Portal" Confusing?

When people discuss Clio, confusion often arises because the word "Portal" refers to two completely different websites built for two completely different audiences:

  • Meaning 1: The Clio Developer Portal (developers.clio.com): A technical control panel for software engineers to register applications, generate OAuth API keys, and read API documentation. Clients and lawyers never use this.
  • Meaning 2: The Clio Client Portal ("Clio for Clients"): A secure mobile and web app where a legal client (e.g., an injured person) logs in to view their court dates, upload documents, and message their lawyer.
  • Versus Clio Grow (grow.clio.com): Neither of the above! Clio Grow is the firm's Sales & Intake CRM, where intake specialists track potential cases on a Kanban board, send intake forms, and collect signed retainer agreements.
Key Distinction in Plain English:
• Developer Portal: The wiring room for coders to get API keys.
• Clio Grow: The sales room for intake staff to turn phone callers into signed clients.
• Clio Manage: The law office where attorneys write legal briefs and track court dates.
• Client Portal: The app on the client's phone to see updates on their case.
