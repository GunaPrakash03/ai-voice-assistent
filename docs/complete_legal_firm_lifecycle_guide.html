Overview: The 4 Life Stages of a Modern Law Firm

To truly understand how this technology works, look at it as 4 sequential life stages:

  1. Stage 1: Setting Up the Law Firm — Configuring the firm's brand, staff roles, and phone number in Clio.
  2. Stage 2: Receiving the Phone Call — A real caller dials the firm's number; the AI Voice Agent talks, questions, and records the intake.
  3. Stage 3: Managing the Case (The Lawyers' Work) — The lawyer reviews the file in Clio Manage, schedules court appearances, and tracks billables.
  4. Stage 4: The Client Portal (The Client's Smartphone Experience) — The client logs in on their phone to see case milestones, upload accident bills, and chat with their lawyer.

Stage 1: How to Set Up the Law Firm in Clio

When you open a law firm account in Clio Manage (https://app.clio.com), you perform four basic setup tasks:

1. Configure Firm Details: Go to Settings > Firm Profile. Enter your firm name (e.g., Apex Legal Group), address, website, and upload your firm logo.
2. Set Up Practice Areas: Go to Settings > Practice Areas. Add your core practice categories: Personal Injury, Car Accidents, Contract Disputes, Family Law.
3. Add Attorney & Staff User Accounts: Go to Settings > Manage Users. Invite your attorneys (e.g., Sarah Jenkins, Lead Counsel) and paralegals with distinct access permissions.
4. Set Up the Firm's Dedicated Phone Number: In your Voice Agent telephony console (or Twilio/Telnyx carrier), obtain a virtual phone number (e.g., +1 (555) 234-5678) and route it to your Voice Agent SIP endpoint.

Stage 3: How the Law Firm Manages the Case in Clio Manage

Once the Voice Agent finishes the call, the attorney never has to do manual data entry. Everything appears in Clio Manage:

What the Firm Staff Does Inside Clio:

  1. Review the File: The paralegal clicks into Matter 00005-Watson, reads the AI-generated executive summary, and listens to the recorded audio.
  2. Assign Onboarding Tasks: With one click, the paralegal applies the firm's "Personal Injury Task Template". Clio automatically assigns:
    • Task 1: Request Police Traffic Incident Report (Due in 3 days).
    • Task 2: Send Medical Authorization HIPPA Release to Mary (Due in 5 days).
  3. Court Calendaring: The attorney enters the court hearing and statute of limitations deadline into Clio's rules-based calendar.
  4. Time Tracking & Billing: The attorney starts Clio's built-in stopwatch timer whenever drafting court documents, logging billable hours automatically.

Stage 4: How the Client Portal Works for the Client ("Clio for Clients")

In traditional firms, clients call every week asking: "Did you get my police report? When is my court date?"
The Clio Client Portal eliminates this completely.

Step 1: The Firm Invites the Client

Inside Clio Manage, on Mary Watson's matter, the attorney clicks "Share with Client Portal". Clio automatically sends Mary an SMS and email with a secure link.

Step 2: What the Client Sees on Her Smartphone

Mary clicks the link and opens the Clio for Clients app on her smartphone:

Step 3: Why Clients Love the Client Portal

  • Instant Document Uploads: When Mary gets a doctor's bill, she takes a photo with her phone and taps upload. The photo saves directly into Clio Manage under her matter's document folder.
  • Secure Messaging: No lost emails or spam folders. Messages are encrypted and stored right inside the case file for attorney-client privilege.
  • Online Payments: Mary can pay court filing fees or retainers using Apple Pay, Google Pay, or debit card directly through the portal via Clio Payments.
