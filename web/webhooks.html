How it works. After every call the AI reads the transcript, fills in the data fields you define per agent (name, phone, matter, …) and the result is posted as extracted in the call.completed webhook, signed with your endpoint secret. Use Suggest from prompt to let the AI propose the fields from the agent's own instructions.
