Key Takeaways & Architectural Advantages of Call Desk

  • Cost Efficiency at Scale: By operating directly on top of LiveKit with BYOK provider keys, Call Desk eliminates the 200%–500% platform margin charged by proprietary vendors (Vapi, Bland). At 50,000 call minutes/mo, savings exceed $4,500/month.
  • Full Telephony & DSP Stack: Call Desk implements complete dual-channel stereo recording (Caller Left / Agent Right), 1400Hz legal compliance beeps, Goertzel dual-tone DSP for DTMF keypad and IVR navigation, and temporal cadence Answering Machine Detection (AMD).
  • Ultra-Low Latency Media Path: Combining Silero VAD, Cartesia Sonic streaming TTS (sub-100ms TTFA), and Deepgram Nova-3 speech recognition provides human-like responsiveness (~500ms turnaround) with instant acoustic barge-in cutoff.
  • Complete Data Sovereignty: All call recordings, transcripts, customer PII, and structured JSON extractions are retained securely in your own PostgreSQL database rather than shared across third-party multi-tenant clouds.
