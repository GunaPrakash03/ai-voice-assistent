Where the money goes

What the numbers do and do not include

  • Run on your server, not per minute: media hosting (LiveKit), turn-taking and interruption handling, the worker, Postgres, the dashboard and call storage. That is the "server per month" line, so it gets cheaper per minute as volume grows.
  • Not counted: engineering and maintenance time, and optional extras such as knowledge bases or PII redaction services.
  • Rate limits: free-tier Gemini keys throttle busy lines; the figures assume paid API tiers.
  • Real phone calls additionally need the LiveKit SIP gateway on a public address; the telephony line above is the carrier's charge once that exists.
